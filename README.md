# 🧠 Smart Research Workspace (SRW)

> **An AI-powered framework for transforming scientific research into a structured, intelligent, and collaborative workflow.**

---

## 🎯 Vision

Smart Research Workspace (SRW) is an open framework designed to help researchers, graduate students, and academic teams manage the entire research lifecycle using Artificial Intelligence.

The goal of SRW is to transform fragmented research activities into a structured, transparent, and reproducible workflow—from idea generation to scientific publication.

---

## ❓ Why SRW?

Modern research often involves multiple disconnected tools, documents, and workflows.

SRW provides a unified research workspace that integrates AI into every stage of the scientific process, enabling researchers to work more efficiently while maintaining academic quality and research integrity.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 📚 AI-assisted literature review
* 💡 Research idea generation and evaluation
* 📝 Scientific writing support
* 📊 Research project management
* 📂 Knowledge organization
* 🤝 Human-AI collaborative research workflow
* 🎯 Publication-oriented methodology
